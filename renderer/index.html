How to use

1. Pick a character. The sidebar lists your characters by name. Click one to load its quickbars. If a character has two specs, Spec 1 / Spec 2 buttons appear underneath — click to switch between them (each spec has its own binds).

2. Edit binds. Each bar shows one bank of 10 slots, like in-game. Click a slot and press the key combo (with Shift, Ctrl, or Alt) to bind it; right-click a slot to clear it. Switch banks with the numbered chips above a bar or by scrolling the mouse wheel over it — a dot under a number means that bank has binds. Expand all banks shows the full 10×10 grid.

3. Save. Nothing is written to disk until you click Save. If you switch to another character, spec, or profile with unsaved edits, you'll be asked to Save, discard, or cancel — so you never lose work by accident.

Profiles. A profile is a reusable set of binds + macros (e.g. "PvE Reaver", "BG Crafter"). Click Save as profile… to save whatever's currently loaded as a new one. Click a profile in the sidebar to open and edit it just like a character — Save writes your changes back to that profile.

Copy to other characters. With any character or profile loaded, click Apply to characters… to copy its binds/macros into other characters you pick. Each target gets a fresh .bak backup first.

Folder. The editor auto-finds your eden folder on first launch. Use the ☰ menu (top-right) → Change eden folder… for a non-standard install; the ↻ and folder buttons beside the character list reload it or open it in Explorer.

Tip: The banner at the top warns you when DAoC is running. Always close the game before saving — the client overwrites the INI on logout.
